## Явления

- **Грокинг / отложенная генерализация (grokking / delayed generalization)** —
  запоминание, затем внезапная генерализация.
  Цитата: [`"delayed generalization after memorization"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p1-2)).
- **Фаза меморизации / плато (memorization phase / plateau)** — которую
  интервенции устраняют.
  Цитата: [`"eliminates the memorization phase entirely on modular addition"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p1-2)
- **Фазовый переход (phase transition)** — резкий скачок test accuracy.
  Цитата: [`"a sudden transition to full generalization after prolonged optimization"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p1-4)).
- **Slingshot effect** — осцилляции/скачки на плато у бейзлайнов.
  Цитата: [`"consistent with the "slingshot effect" reported in prior work"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p9-4)
- **Softmax Collapse** — коллапс из-за неограниченного роста логитов.
  Цитата: [`"unconstrained logit growth leads to Softmax Collapse"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p3-4)
- **Naive Loss Minimization** — раздувание логитов ради снижения потерь.
  Цитата: [`"training under cross-entropy loss can drive increasing logit magnitudes to artificially lower the loss. This behavior—known as Naïve Loss Minimization—leads to numerical instability"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p6-5).

## Механизмы и представления

- **Фурье-признаки / фурье-контур (Fourier features / circuit)** — структура
  генерализующего решения на модульной арифметике.
  Цитата: [`"structured representations based on discrete Fourier features"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p2-1)
- **Алгоритмы "Clock" и "Pizza" (Clock vs Pizza)** — структурное vs
  фрагментированное решение.
  Цитата: [`"the "Pizza" algorithm) rather than the structured, continuous
  Fourier solution (the "Clock""`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p2-2)).
- **Coset circuits / неприводимые представления** — структура решения для S5.
  Цитата: [`"successful $S_{5}$ solutions rely on discrete coset structures"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p2-5)
- **Сжатие многообразия представлений (representation manifold compression)** —
  геометрическая интерпретация задержки.
  Цитата: [`"slow compression of the representation manifold"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p3-3)
- **Максимизация зазора (implicit margin maximization)** — драйвер появления
  структурных контуров.
  Цитата: [`"driven by implicit margin maximization under"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p3-3)

  Цитата: [`"approximate norm minimization on the zero-loss manifold"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p3-3)

## Интервенции и методы

- **Сферическая топология / L2-нормализация (spherical topology)** —
  ограничение residual stream на гиперсферу.
  Цитата: [`"A fully bounded spherical topology, enforcing L2 normalization throughout"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p1-2)).
- **Bounded output / температура (temperature-scaled cosine logits)** —
  нормализованный unembedding с фиксированной tau.
  Цитата: [`"logit magnitudes are strictly bounded by the temperature parameter"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p6-7)).
- **Uniform Attention / bag-of-tokens (CBOW)** — зануление query-key routing.
  Цитата: [`"reducing the attention mechanism to a Continuous Bag-of-Words"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p2-4)
- **Fourier Initialization** — инициализация эмбеддингов синусами/косинусами.
  Цитата: [`"deterministically initialized with cosine and sine values at five key frequencies"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p6-8)).
- **nGPT / гиперсфера** — нормализованный трансформер (смежная работа).
  Цитата: [`"a normalized Transformer that constrains all vectors to the unit hypersphere"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p4-2)).
- **Fourier head** — фурье-ограниченная выходная голова (смежная работа).
  Цитата: [`"Fourier-constrained output heads"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p3-4)).
- **Ускорение через данные / оптимизатор (data augmentation, gradient
  filtering)** — альтернативные подходы к сокращению задержки.
  Цитата: [`"accelerates grokking up to 50× via gradient low-pass filtering"`](../papers/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology/original/2603.05228.the-geometric-inductive-bias-of-grokking-bypassing-phase-transitions-via-architectural-topology.md#p4-1)
